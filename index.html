Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our loan assistance services.

We assist with six major loan categories: New Car Loans, Used Car Loans, Business Loans, Personal Loans, Home Loans, and Loan Against Property (LAP). Our team guides you through the enquiry process for whichever category fits your needs.
No, we are a loan assistance service. We help you explore suitable lending options from banks and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs). The actual loan approval, terms, interest rates, and eligibility are decided entirely by the lending institution.
Our initial enquiry and guidance service is free. We believe in transparent communication, so any applicable charges (if any) will be clearly communicated to you before you proceed further. There are no hidden costs.
While requirements vary by loan type and lender, common documents include identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN), address proof, income proof (salary slips or ITR), bank statements, and property documents for secured loans. Our team will share a detailed checklist based on your specific loan type.
